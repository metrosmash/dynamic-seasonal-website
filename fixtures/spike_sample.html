A Supper by Lantern Light

When the evenings turn crisp, we gather around the long table for a meal made from the last gifts of the garden.

Gather what the garden gives

Roasted squash, sweet onions, and a handful of herbs make a simple beginning. Let the vegetables take their time in a warm oven until their edges turn golden.

Set a welcoming table

Place a small lantern at the center of the table, add a few fallen leaves, and serve the soup while it is still steaming.

Good food is best when there is room for one more at the table.

Leave room for something sweet

Finish with baked apples, cinnamon, and a spoonful of cream. Share stories while the kettle warms for one last cup of tea.
